Why Forge

A gym, not a gimmick.

No mirrors turned into content sets, no colour-coded juice bar. Forge is built around three things: heavy equipment that doesn't wobble, coaches who correct your form uninvited, and a floor that smells like chalk, not eucalyptus.

Whether you're chasing your first pull-up or your fifth powerlifting meet, you train next to people doing the same — not past them on a screen.

  • 01Certified strength coaches on the floor at all times, not just in classes.
  • 02Olympic platforms, competition bars, and a dedicated conditioning bay.
  • 03Small class caps — 12 max — so coaching actually happens.
  • 04Open 5 AM to 11 PM, because life doesn't run on gym hours.
Programs

Pick your discipline

Four tracks, one floor. Mix and match as your membership allows.

Strength

Powerbuilding

Squat, bench, deadlift progressions with accessory work for size. Coached, logged, and tested every 8 weeks.

Conditioning

Metcon Floor

High-output circuits on sleds, rowers, and bikes. Built for engine, not for Instagram.

Combat

Bag & Pad Work

Boxing fundamentals, pad rounds, and heavy-bag conditioning led by a former amateur boxer.

Recovery

Mobility Lab

Joint prep, band work, and breathing drills so the other three programs don't wreck you.
